What You Can Contact Us About

  • Help using the Owner App or the Customer App
  • Login, PIN or sync problems
  • Privacy questions about either app
  • Account deletion requests
  • Reporting a wrong or misleading room listing

To Help Us Reply Faster, Please Include

  • Which app you use: Owner App or Customer App
  • The email address linked to your account
  • A short description of the problem, and a screenshot if you can
  • Your phone model and Android version

Please do not send your app PIN or passwords by email. We will never ask for them.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account inside either app, or email us from your registered email address. See the Account Deletion page for the steps.

Disputes Between Owners and Customers

Rent, deposits, bills and complaints are matters between the property owner and the customer. Please contact the owner or tenant directly about them. We can help with problems in the Apps themselves.
