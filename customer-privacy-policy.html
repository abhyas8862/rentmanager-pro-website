Customer App Privacy Policy

RentManager Pro Customer App

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the RentManager Pro Customer app (“the App”, package com.abhyas.rentmanager.customer) handles information. The App lets you search vacant rooms, send booking inquiries, and, if you are a tenant, view your bills, pay rent by UPI and raise complaints. Property owners use a separate app, covered by the Owner App Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: your name and email address from Google Sign-In or email login.
  • Profile details: information you enter, such as your name and mobile number.
  • Booking inquiries: the room you ask about, your name, contact details and any message you write.
  • Tenant link: the Unique Tenant ID (RM-XXXXXXXX) you enter to link your account to a rented room.
  • Rental records: bills, receipts, payment history and notices for your linked tenancy, created by your owner.
  • Payments you report: amount, date and reference of UPI payments you tell the owner about.
  • Complaints: the text and status of complaints you file.
  • App settings: your chosen language and app PIN, stored on your device.

2. How We Use Information

To sign you in, show vacant rooms, deliver your booking inquiries to owners, link you to your tenancy after the owner approves, show your bills and receipts, record payments and complaints, show notices, lock the App with your PIN, and respond to support or deletion requests.

3. Who Can See Your Information

  • Owners you contact: an owner can see your booking inquiry. After the owner accepts it, your contact details are available to that owner, and the owner’s mobile number is shown to you.
  • Your linked owner: once you are linked, the owner can see your profile details, payments you report and complaints you file.
  • Other customers: other customers cannot see your personal information.

Room listings you browse are published by property owners and are visible to all customers.

4. Payments

The App does not process payments and does not use a payment gateway. When you pay rent, the payment happens in a UPI app of your choice, under that app’s own terms and privacy policy. You may then report the payment in the App, and the owner confirms it. We do not collect your bank account, card or UPI PIN details.

5. Storage and Third-Party Services

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Sign-in is handled by Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication, under their own privacy policies. Your app PIN and language choice are stored on your device. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Retention

Information is kept while your account is active and as needed to provide the App’s features. Bills, receipts and payment records belong to the property owner’s rental records, and the owner may keep them after you unlink, as needed for their own accounts or legal requirements.

7. Unlinking and Account Deletion

  • Unlink: you can unlink from a tenancy in your profile.
  • Delete account: you can delete your account from your profile inside the App, or email us to request deletion. Your account data and inquiries in the App’s cloud storage will be removed.

Records the owner already holds about your tenancy, and anything you exported or shared outside the App, are not removed by deleting your customer account.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device, email account and app PIN private. If you forget your PIN, use the Forgot PIN option in the App.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not designed for children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the App or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, email rentManagerproenterprises@gmail.com.
