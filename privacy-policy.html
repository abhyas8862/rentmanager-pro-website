Privacy Policy

RentManager Pro Enterprise (Owner App)

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RentManager Pro Enterprise (“the App”, the owner app) handles information when you use its rental-management features. Please read it before entering personal or financial records. A separate policy covers the RentManager Pro Customer app.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, information entered into the App may include:

  • account and sign-in information (name, email, Google account details);
  • owner profile details, property and room details, and your UPI ID and mobile number;
  • tenant names, contact details and Unique Tenant IDs;
  • rental records, bills, payments, advances, outstanding balances and expenses;
  • room-listing details (title, area, city, rent, advance, sale price, description, facilities) and room photos;
  • complaints, notices and booking inquiries exchanged with customers;
  • information contained in documents or photos you choose to add.

Please ensure that you have permission to enter and manage information about other people, including tenants.

2. Account and Sign-In Information

The App offers sign-in with Google. Sign-in is handled by Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication, which process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. The App also offers an app PIN, which is stored on your device and is used only to lock the App.

3. Local Storage and Cloud Synchronization

Some records are stored on your device so that the App can work. When you are signed in, supported records are also stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your account so they can be restored after login. Only you can access your own records through the account rules set for the App.

Local and cloud storage are separate. Deleting a record in one place does not always delete every other copy.

4. Room Listings and the Customer App

If you publish a vacant room, its listing details (such as title, area, city, rent, advance, description and facilities) are stored in the cloud and become visible to customers using the RentManager Pro Customer app, including customers who are not your tenants.

  • Your mobile number is shown to a customer only after you accept that customer’s booking inquiry.
  • A booking inquiry does not book a room automatically. You decide whether to accept it.
  • Room photos added in the App are currently kept on your device and are not saved to the cloud.
  • You can unpublish a room at any time. A room is marked vacant automatically when a tenant’s account is closed.

5. Tenant Linking, Bills, Complaints and Payments

  • A customer can link to a tenant record only by entering the Unique Tenant ID and receiving your approval. You can unlink a customer.
  • For a linked customer, the App shares bills, receipts, payment history and notices you create for that tenant, so the customer can view them in the Customer app.
  • Complaints filed by linked customers are stored in the cloud and shown in the Complaints section of the App. Status updates you make are visible to the customer.
  • The App does not process payments and does not use a payment gateway. A customer may report a UPI payment, and you confirm it yourself. UPI apps used for the actual payment are governed by their own policies.

6. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide rental-management functions, maintain tenant and property records, prepare bills and receipts, calculate balances, record payments and expenses, synchronize records, publish room listings you choose to publish, connect you with customers, protect accounts, fix technical problems, and respond to support or deletion requests.

7. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal information. Google (Sign-In) and Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) process information needed for login and cloud storage. Information is shared with customers only as described in sections 4 and 5.

If you print, export or share a bill, receipt, photo or other record using another app (for example WhatsApp), that service processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. Please check the destination before sharing sensitive information.

8. Data Retention

Information is kept for as long as needed to provide the App’s features and keep your records, or as required for legal or security reasons. Retention can differ between your device and the cloud.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account inside the App from More → Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account data from the cloud and from your device, including your published room listings. You can also read the steps on our Account Deletion page or email us to request deletion.

Deletion does not remove exported files, screenshots, printed documents or copies already shared through other services, such as PDFs you sent on WhatsApp. Tenant-related copies held by customers in their own app are covered by the Customer app policy.

Export any records you may need before deleting your account.

10. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used through the App and its configured services to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device, Google account and app PIN safe.

11. Children’s Privacy

The App is meant for property owners and is not designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

12. Your Choices and Responsibilities

You are responsible for the information you enter and for having permission to store or manage it. You can choose what to provide, unpublish rooms, unlink customers, and delete your account. Uninstalling the App does not delete information already synchronized to the cloud.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the App or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, email rentManagerproenterprises@gmail.com.
