Privacy Policy

RentManager Pro Enterprise
Last updated: 27 October 2026

Publication checklist: This is a draft policy and must be checked against the app's current code, Firebase configuration, analytics/diagnostics SDKs and actual deletion workflow before publication or submission to Google Play. Remove this notice only after that verification is complete.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RentManager Pro Enterprise (“the App”) may handle information when you use it. The App is intended to help users maintain rental-management records, such as tenant details, bills, payments, expenses and complaints.

2. Information you enter

Depending on the features you use, information you enter may include account and sign-in details; owner or property details; tenant names and contact details; rental, billing, payment, advance/balance and expense records; complaint records; and information included in documents or photos you choose to add. Please do not enter information that you do not have permission to store.

3. Account and authentication

The App supports account sign-in features. Firebase Authentication may be used to authenticate users, depending on the sign-in method selected. Authentication providers may process information under their own privacy terms. The exact account fields and providers enabled in the released build should be verified before this policy is finalized.

4. Local storage and cloud synchronization

Some app records may be stored locally on your device. If you use cloud synchronization, selected app records may be transferred to and stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore associated with your account. The specific tables, fields, images and files synchronized should be confirmed from the current app code before publication.

5. How information is used

Information is used to provide app features, maintain rental and payment records, calculate balances, generate bills or receipts, synchronize records across supported sessions/devices, protect accounts, troubleshoot issues and respond to support requests.

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate features you choose, such as Firebase authentication and cloud storage. If you export, print or share a bill, receipt or other record through another app (for example, a messaging or printing app), that action is handled by the selected third-party service under its own terms.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records may remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the App; uninstalling may not delete cloud records. Cloud records and account data should be deleted through the App's account-deletion process or by contacting us as described on the Account Deletion page. The actual deletion workflow and retention of backups must be verified before this policy is published. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Security

We use the security features available in the app platform and configured services to help protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Users should protect their device, account credentials and PIN, and should not share them with others.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Your choices

You can choose what information to enter, use available app controls to manage records, and request account deletion. Removing the App from your device may not remove information already synchronized to the cloud.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App changes. The “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email rentmanagerproenterprises@gmail.com.
