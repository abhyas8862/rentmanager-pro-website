Privacy Policy

RentManager Pro Enterprise

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RentManager Pro Enterprise (“the App”) handles information when you use its rental-management features. Please read it carefully before entering personal or financial records.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, information entered into the App may include account and sign-in information, owner and property details, tenant names and contact details, rental records, bills, payments, advances, outstanding balances, expenses, complaints, and information contained in documents or photos you choose to add.

Please ensure that you have the appropriate permission to enter and manage information about other people, including tenants.

2. Account and Sign-In Information

The App provides account sign-in features. Information required to authenticate your account may be processed by the authentication provider used for your selected sign-in method. Third-party authentication providers handle information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

3. Local Storage and Cloud Synchronization

Some records may be stored on your device so that the App can provide its features. If cloud synchronization is enabled and used, supported records may be transferred to and stored in the cloud service associated with your account.

Local storage and cloud storage are separate. Deleting a record from one location does not necessarily delete every other copy. The information synchronized depends on the features and configuration of the App.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide rental-management functions, maintain tenant and property records, prepare bills and receipts, calculate balances, record payments and expenses, synchronize supported records, protect accounts, address technical problems, and respond to support or deletion requests.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal information. Services used to provide account authentication or cloud synchronization may process information necessary for those features.

If you choose to print, export, or share a bill, receipt, photo, or other record using another application or service, that service may process the information you share under its own terms and privacy policy. Please review the destination before sharing sensitive information.

6. Data Retention

Information may remain stored for as long as it is needed to provide the App's features, maintain your records, meet applicable legal requirements, or address legitimate security matters. The retention period can differ between information stored on your device and information stored in the cloud.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can review the available account-deletion procedure on our Account Deletion page.

Deleting your account may not automatically remove information stored only on your device, exported files, screenshots, printed documents, or copies held by third-party services. Where applicable, certain information may be retained for legal or security reasons.

Before deleting an account or local app data, export any records you are legally entitled to keep and may need in the future.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used through the App and its configured services to help protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Keep your device, account credentials, and app PIN secure. Do not share authentication details or grant access to people who are not authorized to view your records.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for rental-management purposes and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

10. Your Choices and Responsibilities

You are responsible for the information you enter and for ensuring that you have permission to store or manage it. You can choose what information to provide, use available App features to manage records, and follow the account-deletion procedure when you wish to request deletion.

Uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete information that has already been synchronized to a cloud service.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the App, its features, or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
