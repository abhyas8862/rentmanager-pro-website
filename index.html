Owner App: Powerful Features for Better Management

RentManager Pro Enterprise helps you organize everyday rental-management tasks.

⌂

Property & Tenant Records

Organize property details, tenant information and contact records.

₹

Bills & Payments

Maintain rental bills, payment entries, advances and balances.

▤

Expenses & Complaints

Keep expense and complaint records organized.

☁

Cloud Synchronization

Use cloud synchronization to keep supported records associated with your account.

▥

Reports & Summaries

Review available rental, payment and expense reports.

♧

Login & App PIN

Use the available account sign-in and PIN features to access the App.

▧

Bills & PDF Receipts

Prepare available billing and receipt documents for your records.

⇧

Print & Share

Use available print and sharing options for supported documents.

⌂

Rooms & Vacancy

Publish vacant rooms for customers to find. A room shows as vacant when a tenant account is closed.

Customer Link & Approval

Review booking inquiries and approve customers who link with a Unique Tenant ID.

UPI QR & Payment Confirmation

Show your UPI QR on bills and confirm payments reported by customers.

Customer App: Find Rooms and Stay Connected

RentManager Pro Customer helps you find rooms and manage your tenancy with your owner.

⌕

Room Finder

Search vacant rooms and flats published by property owners.

✉

Booking Inquiry

Send an inquiry to the owner. The owner’s mobile number is shown after the owner accepts.

♙

Link with Tenant ID

Link your account to your rented room using your Unique Tenant ID, with owner approval.

⌂

My Home

See your rent, this month’s bill and payment history in one place.

▧

Bills & Receipts

View your bill history and receipts for your records.

₹

UPI Payment Report

Pay through your UPI app, then report the payment. The owner confirms it.

▤

Complaints & Notices

Raise complaints, follow their status and read notices from your owner.

♧

Login, PIN & Languages

Sign in with Google or email, lock the App with a PIN and choose your language.
