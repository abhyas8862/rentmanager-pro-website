Account Deletion

RentManager Pro Enterprise and RentManager Pro Customer

Last updated: 14 November 2026

You can delete your account and data from either app. This page covers both apps and explains what is deleted and what is not.

Owner App (RentManager Pro Enterprise)

Delete inside the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Read the warning and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and all records synced to the cloud (tenants, bills, payments, expenses, complaints, owner profile).
  • The same data stored on your phone.
  • Your published room listings.

Customer App (RentManager Pro Customer)

Delete inside the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to your Profile and choose Delete Account.
  3. Confirm.

To stop sharing with an owner without deleting your account, choose Unlink in your profile.

What is deleted

  • Your customer account and profile details.
  • Your booking inquiries and your link to a tenancy.
  • App settings stored on your phone, such as language and PIN.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, email rentManagerproenterprises@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Write the app name (Owner App or Customer App) and the words “Delete my account”. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will delete your data within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

What may remain

  • Files you exported or shared outside the apps, such as PDFs, screenshots and WhatsApp messages.
  • For a customer: bills, receipts, payments and complaints that your owner already holds as part of the owner’s own rental records.
  • Information we must keep for legal or security reasons.

Deleting an account cannot be undone. Export anything you need before you delete.
