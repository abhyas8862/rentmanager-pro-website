Delete Your Account in the App

If you can sign in to RentManager Pro Enterprise, open the account or profile section in the app and use the Delete Account option. Review the confirmation prompt carefully before proceeding.

Account deletion is intended to remove your Firebase Authentication account and associated cloud records covered by the app's deletion process. Please ensure that you have exported any information you want to keep before confirming deletion.

Request Account Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app or need help with account deletion, contact us from an email address you can access. Identify your account using the email address associated with it.

Use the subject line RentManager Pro Account Deletion. Do not send your password or PIN.

Email Account Deletion Request

We will review the request, verify that it is authorized, and respond with the next steps. Emailing a request does not itself mean that deletion has already been completed.

What Data Is Deleted?

The in-app deletion process is intended to delete the Firebase Authentication account and associated cloud records covered by that process. Data stored locally on your device may require separate removal.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes. If applicable, such retention should be explained to the requester.

Local Data and Exported Files

Data stored only on your phone may remain until you remove the app's local data or uninstall the app. Exported PDFs, backups, screenshots, and files shared through other apps may remain outside our control.

Deleting your account does not automatically delete copies that you have saved or shared outside the app.
