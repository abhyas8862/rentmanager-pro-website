Account Deletion

RentManager Pro Enterprise

Important: This page is a draft. Before publishing, confirm that the app has a working in-app deletion option and implement a reliable process for handling requests received by email. Do not claim that deletion is complete until it has actually been performed.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your RentManager Pro Enterprise account, email rentmanagerproenterprises@gmail.com from an address you can access.

Use the subject “RentManager Pro Account Deletion”. In your message, identify the account using the email address associated with it. Do not send your password or PIN.

What happens next

We will verify that the request is authorized and provide next steps. Account deletion should include deleting the account and associated personal data from the systems under our control, subject to applicable legal requirements. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes; any such retention should be explained to the requester.

Local data and exports

Data stored only on your device may need to be deleted separately from within the App or by clearing its app data. Deleting the app from your phone does not necessarily delete cloud-stored records. Exported files, PDFs, screenshots and records shared through other apps may remain outside our control.

Contact

For account deletion questions, email rentmanagerproenterprises@gmail.com.
